Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Who we are

Doodle Courier ("the app") is a route-drawing puzzle game developed by Sadık Çoban ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes and why. Questions: cobansadik99@gmail.com.

No account, data stays with you

You don't create an account. Your progress, stars, coins, lives and settings are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, synced to your own iCloud account through Apple so they survive reinstalls and appear on your other devices. We can't access this data.

Services that process data

Google AdMob (ads): shows ads and measures their performance. It may process your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads.

Apple App Store: purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.

Apple iCloud: stores your game progress in your own iCloud account, as described above.

Your choices

Apple asks whether the app may track you. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

You can remove ads between levels with the Remove Ads purchase. Reward videos are always optional.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the providers above, under their own privacy policies: Google (policies.google.com/privacy), Google partners (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and Apple (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Data in your iCloud account stays until you remove it in your iCloud settings. Google keeps ad data according to its own policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. For anything else, write to cobansadik99@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
